who's the person behind this?

I'm an amateur birdwatcher based in Jeju, Korea. i started birding to help me relax and take my mind off the adversity of everyday life.

Once, my friend and i were taking a walk and there was a bird on the side of the road, breathing heavily and trying to fly away in vain. it was hit by a car. it back to my friend's house in my school bag, and we carefully transferred it to a cardboard box. we contacted the wildlife rescue center, and the bird was taken to the clinic. we were later informed that the bird passed away while preparing for surgery due to a broken coracoid bone. from that day, i desired to contribute to protecting and preserving birds from the anthropogenic threats such as habitat destruction, road collisions, and climate change.
